API Setup - Create Your Own Spotify App

1 Open Spotify Developer Dashboard

Go to the Spotify Developer Dashboard and sign in with your Spotify account:

→ Open Spotify Dashboard
2 Create New App
  1. Click "Create app"
  2. App Name: Cxeify (or any name)
  3. App Description: Local Spotify Controller
  4. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:3000/callback
  5. Website: Leave empty (optional)
  6. Select: "Web API"
  7. Accept the Terms of Service
  8. Click "Save"
💡 Important: The Redirect URI must be exactly http://127.0.0.1:3000/callback!
3 Copy API Credentials
  1. In your newly created app, click "Settings"
  2. Copy the Client ID
  3. Click "View client secret" and copy the Client Secret
⚠️ Important: The Client Secret is confidential! Never share it publicly.
4 Enter Credentials (Session Only)
🔒 Security Note:
Your credentials are saved locally in your AppData folder. They are required for automatic token refresh. Your credentials never leave your computer.
✓ Credentials set! Redirecting to Spotify authorization...
ℹ️ What happens next:
You'll be redirected to Spotify to authorize the app. After authorization, your access token will be saved and automatically refreshed when needed. You only need to do this setup once!
